## About the lab

The Bald lab studies how immune cells and cancer cells talk to each other inside tumours. We use preclinical
cancer models and patient-derived material to find the receptors, cell states and signals that decide whether an
anti-tumour immune response works, with the aim of identifying targets that can be drugged. Melanoma is a major
model; we also work on kidney, lung, bladder, prostate and haematological cancers, mostly through collaborations.
A current focus is the **activating receptors** that let NK cells and T cells recognise and kill tumour cells, and
the ways tumours switch them off.

The lab is led by [Prof. Tobias Bald](https://www.immunosensation.de/members/prof-dr-tobias-bald), a member and
steering-committee member of the **ImmunoSensation³** Cluster of Excellence, and sits in the Institute of
Experimental Oncology at the University Hospital Bonn.

## Research programmes

### 1. Activating receptors and the CD155 axis
Tumour cells display the ligand CD155, which engages the activating receptor DNAM-1 (CD226) and the inhibitory
receptor TIGIT. We showed that CD155 on tumour cells drives Src-dependent phosphorylation, CBL-B ubiquitination and
degradation of CD226 on CD8⁺ T cells, which makes tumours resistant to immunotherapy. Related work links tumour CD155
to anti-PD-1 resistance in patients, to IL-22 from T cells, and to extracellular vesicles that strip DNAM-1 from NK
cells.
→ [Braun *et al.*, Immunity 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2020.09.010) ·
[Lepletier *et al.*, Clin Cancer Res 2020](https://doi.org/10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-19-3925) ·
[Briukhovetska *et al.*, Immunity 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2022.12.010) ·
[Wu *et al.*, Int J Mol Sci 2026](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms27198699)

### 2. NK cells and innate lymphoid cells in cancer
NK cells and their relatives are plastic. TGF-β signalling in tumours can convert cytotoxic NK cells into
less cytotoxic type 1 innate lymphoid cells (ILC1) that no longer control tumour growth. We study this plasticity, the
cytotoxic machinery of NK cells (including the granule protein NKG7), and how disseminated tumour cells disable NK
cells in metastasis.
→ [Gao *et al.*, Nat Immunol 2017](https://doi.org/10.1038/ni.3800) ·
[Ng *et al.*, Nat Immunol 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41590-020-0758-6) ·
[Li *et al.*, Cancer Immunol Res 2022](https://doi.org/10.1158/2326-6066.CIR-20-0649) ·
[Corvino *et al.*, Front Immunol 2022](https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2022.888313) ·
[Pedde *et al.*, Cell Rep 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2024.114855) ·
[Turiello *et al.*, Eur J Immunol 2025](https://doi.org/10.1002/eji.202551885)

### 3. Melanoma, inflammation and resistance to immunotherapy
Melanoma cells escape T-cell therapy by changing state, and the inflammatory environment around them (neutrophils,
interferon, UV-driven inflammation) decides how well therapy works. We follow these programmes with adoptive T-cell
transfer, single-cell and spatial profiling.
→ [Landsberg *et al.*, Nature 2012](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature11538) ·
[Bald *et al.*, Nature 2014](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature13111) ·
[Glodde *et al.*, Immunity 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2017.09.012) ·
[Effern *et al.*, Immunity 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2020.07.007) ·
[Yam *et al.*, Cancer Res 2023](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-21-4025) ·
[van der Voort *et al.*, npj Syst Biol Appl 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41540-026-00765-5)

### 4. T-cell states and interferon signalling
Chronic stimulation and type I interferon push T cells into states that respond poorly to TCR signals and to
therapy. We map these states and test which signals can be changed.
→ [Corvino *et al.*, Eur J Immunol 2024](https://doi.org/10.1002/eji.202451371) ·
[Pretto *et al.*, Nat Metab 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42255-025-01233-w)

### 5. Biomarkers and translational oncology
With clinical partners we look for blood and tissue markers that predict who benefits from checkpoint blockade or
targeted therapy, and for drug-resistance mechanisms we can act on.
→ [Klümper *et al.*, J Immunother Cancer 2022](https://doi.org/10.1136/jitc-2021-004024) ·
[Saal *et al.*, JAMA Oncol 2023](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaoncol.2023.1822) ·
[Hosni *et al.*, Cancer Res 2024](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-23-1398) ·
[Weiten *et al.*, Cancer Med 2024](https://doi.org/10.1002/cam4.7320)

### 6. New tools
Optical perturbation screens and AI-guided protein design let us test targets faster.
→ [Fandrey *et al.*, Nat Biotechnol 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-024-02516-5) ·
[Broske *et al.*, Nat Commun 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-76760-5)

## Open code and data

We publish analysis code with our papers, with a licence, a citation file and a permanent Zenodo DOI. Lab
repositories stay private until the paper is out; released code lives under
[github.com/Eomesodermin](https://github.com/Eomesodermin).

| Paper | Code |
|---|---|
| Braun *et al.*, Immunity 2020 (CD155, CD226 degradation) | [Braun_et_al_Immunity_2020](https://github.com/Eomesodermin/Braun_et_al_Immunity_2020) |
| Hosni, Kilian *et al.*, Cancer Res 2024 (NRG1, FGFR inhibition) | [Hosni_Kilian_etal_Cancer_Research_2023](https://github.com/Eomesodermin/Hosni_Kilian_etal_Cancer_Research_2023) |
| Corvino *et al.*, Eur J Immunol 2024 (type I interferon, T-cell state) | [Corvino_etal_2024_EJI](https://github.com/Eomesodermin/Corvino_etal_2024_EJI) |
| Turiello, Ng *et al.*, Eur J Immunol 2025 (NKG7) | [Turiello_Ng_etal_EJI_2025](https://github.com/Eomesodermin/Turiello_Ng_etal_EJI_2025) |

Shared resources: [r-utility-functions](https://github.com/Eomesodermin/r-utility-functions) (R helpers for
single-cell and bulk analysis), [lab-protocols](https://github.com/Eomesodermin/lab-protocols),
[HPC_workflows](https://github.com/Eomesodermin/HPC_workflows), and reanalyses of public NK-cell, ILC1 and tumour
immune-cell datasets ([NK and ILC1](https://github.com/Eomesodermin/nk-ilc1-scrnaseq-reanalyses),
[tumour immune cells](https://github.com/Eomesodermin/tumour-immune-scrnaseq-reanalyses),
[reference atlases](https://github.com/Eomesodermin/reference-atlas-reanalyses)).
